## Executive Summary

Tulsa has a vibrant ecosystem of independent medical practices across primary care, dental, chiropractic, mental health, and dermatology. These practices share common pain points ideal for Doer:

- **Front desk inefficiency**: Manual scheduling and call handling
- **After-hours coverage gap**: Patients reach voicemail after 5pm
- **No-show revenue loss**: Industry average 10-30% no-show rates
- **Administrative burden**: Small practices lack dedicated admin staff
- **Patient acquisition**: Competition from hospital-owned systems

**Key Insight:** Many Tulsa practices emphasize "personalized care" and "old school medicine" as differentiators against corporate healthcare — this messaging aligns perfectly with Doer's value proposition of human-like AI that maintains the personal touch.

## CONTACT STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS

### Best Days/Times to Call:
- **Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-11:30am or 2pm-4pm** — Avoid morning rush and lunch
- **Avoid Monday mornings** — Practices catching up from weekend
- **Avoid Friday afternoons** — Staff checking out for weekend

### Decision Maker Approach:
- **Solo Practices:** Speak directly to owner/doctor — they're wearing all hats
- **Group Practices:** Ask for Practice Manager or Office Manager
- **Dentists:** Office Manager often handles software decisions
- **Chiropractors:** Owner usually makes tech decisions directly

### Opening Lines:
- "Hi, I'm calling about helping practices like yours handle patient calls and scheduling more efficiently..."
- "I work with independent medical practices in Tulsa to reduce front desk burden and capture after-hours appointments..."
- "Are you the person who handles practice efficiency and patient scheduling decisions?"

### Objection Handling:
- **"We already have an answering service"** → "Do they book appointments directly into your system, or just take messages? Doer can actually schedule patients, send reminders, and integrate with your existing software."
- **"We don't get that many calls"** → "Even a few missed calls are lost revenue. And what about after-hours? Patients often call when your office is closed."
- **"We're too small to need that"** → "Actually, smaller practices benefit most — you don't have dedicated front desk coverage for every hour, and every patient matters more."
- **"We prefer the personal touch"** → "Doer IS personal — it answers calls like a human, has natural conversations, and never sounds like a robot. Your patients get the same personalized experience, just 24/7."
