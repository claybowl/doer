## Executive Summary

Tulsa's legal market has a strong ecosystem of independent law firms across personal injury, family law, criminal defense, and general practice. Unlike medical practices, law firms have **higher-value use cases** for Doer:

- **Client intake volume**: High volume of potential client calls (especially PI and criminal defense)
- **Document drafting**: Repetitive templates (contracts, pleadings, discovery responses)
- **Research tasks**: Precedent research, case law review
- **After-hours coverage**: Arrests and accidents happen 24/7 — missed calls = lost cases
- **Paralegal costs**: $45k-$65k/year per paralegal vs. AI agent at fraction of cost

**Key Insight:** Personal injury and criminal defense firms operate on **contingency/flat fee models** — they lose money on every missed call. This creates urgent need for 24/7 intake. Family law firms face emotional clients who need immediate response. Both are ideal Doer targets.

## KEY DIFFERENCES FROM MEDICAL PRACTICES

| Factor | Medical Practices | Law Firms |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Call Urgency** | Important (appointments) | Critical (arrests, accidents) |
| **Revenue per Case** | $100-$500 (visit) | $5k-$500k+ (case value) |
| **After-Hours Need** | Nice to have | Essential (24/7) |
| **Client Acquisition** | Referrals, insurance | Intake response speed |
| **Pain Point** | Efficiency | Case capture |
| **ROI Proposition** | Reduce no-shows | Capture high-value cases |

**Key Insight:** Law firms have **higher urgency** and **higher value per lead** than medical practices. One missed call could be a $50k case. The pitch should emphasize **case capture** and **revenue protection**, not just efficiency.
